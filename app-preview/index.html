ⓘ A note on blood pressure and African/Afro-descent heart health

The categories and thresholds used in this app (Excellent → Critical) are the same standard clinical thresholds used for everyone — there is no separate "normal" blood pressure range by ethnicity, so your readings are graded exactly as a doctor would grade them.

What is well documented: people of African descent tend to develop high blood pressure earlier, more often, and face a higher risk of complications (stroke, kidney disease, heart failure) at the same blood pressure level compared to the general population. That makes consistent tracking like this especially worthwhile.

On medication: older guidance suggested calcium channel blockers or thiazide diuretics as preferred first-line options specifically for Black patients. The 2025 AHA/ACC hypertension guideline moved away from race-based prescribing — treatment is now chosen using individual risk factors (via the race-neutral PREVENT risk calculator) rather than race alone. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about what best fits your personal history, kidney function, and other conditions.

How to analyze it with AI, whenever you need to
  1. Click Download JSON above — it saves a file to your computer, nothing is sent anywhere automatically.
  2. Open Claude (claude.ai, the desktop app, or this project) and start a new conversation.
  3. Attach the downloaded file to your message.
  4. Ask a specific question — for example:
"Does my Amlodipin dose correlate with lower systolic readings over the last month?"
"Do my headaches or dizziness line up with high blood pressure days?"
"Is my blood pressure trending up or down over the last 90 days, and at what time of day is it highest?"

Before you start tracking

NchQuest Health is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and nothing it shows you is medical advice. The wellness colors and scores reflect general clinical blood pressure thresholds, not a personal assessment of your health.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your blood pressure, medication, or any symptoms — and seek emergency care for anything that concerns you rather than relying on this app. Never start, stop, or change a medication based on this app without talking to your doctor or pharmacist.

Everything you log, and anything read from Health Connect, stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded unless you export it yourself. Read the full privacy policy.
