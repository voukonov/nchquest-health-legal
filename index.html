Medical Disclaimer

NchQuest Health is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition, and it does not provide medical advice. The wellness categories, colors, and scores shown in the app (Excellent → Critical) reflect general clinical blood pressure thresholds, not a personal medical assessment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your blood pressure, medication, symptoms, or any change in your health, and seek emergency care for any symptoms that concern you rather than relying on this app. Do not start, stop, or change any medication based on information from this app without talking to your doctor or pharmacist first.

1. Overview

NchQuest Health is a personal blood pressure, medication, symptom, and activity tracker built for one purpose: helping you understand your own health data over time and share it with your doctor if you choose to. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does with your data. If a sentence here doesn't match what the app actually does, treat the app's real behavior as an error to report, not the intended design — this document is written to be strictly accurate to the current release.

The app does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifiers. There is no account or sign-in, so there is nothing tying this data to an identity beyond the device it's stored on.

3. Where your data lives (and where it doesn't go)

  • Everything you enter, and everything read from Health Connect, is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.
  • The app has no backend server and does not sync, upload, or transmit your health data anywhere — not to NchQuest, not to any third party, not to any analytics or advertising service.
  • There is no cloud backup of this data beyond whatever your device's own OS-level backup does with app data generally (standard Android behavior for any app, governed by your own Google account settings, not by NchQuest).
  • The app contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs that would transmit usage or device data off your phone.

4. Android Health Connect access

NchQuest Health integrates with Android Health Connect, the OS-level store Android uses to let apps share fitness and health data with your explicit permission. Today, the app requests read-only access to three data types: Steps, Heart Rate, and Resting Heart Rate. The app does not write any data back to Health Connect, and does not request access to any other Health Connect data type beyond what's listed here.

You grant this access explicitly through the Android permission dialog the first time you tap "Connect" in the Activity tab, and you can review or revoke it at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Health Connect → App permissions, or via the "Open Health Connect setup" option inside the app. Revoking access simply stops the Activity tab from showing new data — it does not delete anything you've already entered manually elsewhere in the app.

5. Network activity

The app requests the Android INTERNET permission for one narrow purpose: loading the open-source Chart.js charting library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) so the trend chart can render. This is a standard library file, not a request that includes any of your personal or health data — no readings, notes, or medication data are sent in that request. Aside from this one library fetch, the app makes no network requests: no data upload, no telemetry, no ad calls.

6. Your control over your data

  • Export: the Export button lets you download your data as CSV or JSON at any time, for your own records or to share with a healthcare provider or an AI assistant of your choosing.
  • Delete a reading: any logged entry can be deleted individually from the History table.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage via Android Settings → Apps → NchQuest Health → Storage → Clear storage, permanently removes all locally stored data. Because nothing is stored remotely, this is a complete and permanent deletion — there is no server-side copy left behind.

7. Children's privacy

NchQuest Health is not directed at children and is not designed for use by anyone under 18. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If NchQuest Health ever adds functionality that changes what data is collected or where it goes — for example, an optional cloud sync feature in a future release — this policy will be updated first, the Play Console Data Safety form will be updated to match, and the in-app disclaimer will reflect the change before it ships.
